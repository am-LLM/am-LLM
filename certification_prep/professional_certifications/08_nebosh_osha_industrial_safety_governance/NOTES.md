## 1. Hierarchy of Risk Controls (ANSI/ASSP Z590.3 / ISO 45001)

```
                       HIERARCHY OF RISK CONTROLS
 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐  ▲ Most Effective
 │ 1. ELIMINATION: Physically remove the hazard                │  │
 ├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤  │
 │ 2. SUBSTITUTION: Replace hazard with safer alternative      │  │
 ├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤  │
 │ 3. ENGINEERING CONTROLS: Isolate workers from the hazard    │  │
 ├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤  │
 │ 4. ADMINISTRATIVE CONTROLS: Change work procedures/training │  │
 ├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤  │
 │ 5. PPE: Protect worker with Personal Protective Equipment   │  │ Least Effective
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘  ▼
```

## 2. Quantitative Safety Metrics & OSHA Recordkeeping Formulas

### Total Recordable Incident Rate ($TRIR$)
Measures the number of recordable injuries and illnesses per 100 full-time employees per year ($200,000$ employee hours base):
$$TRIR = \frac{\text{Total Recordable Incidents (N)} \times 200,000}{\text{Total Employee Hours Worked (EH)}}$$

### Days Away, Restricted, or Transferred Rate ($DART$)
$$DART = \frac{(\text{Days Away} + \text{Restricted} + \text{Job Transfer Incidents}) \times 200,000}{\text{Total Employee Hours Worked (EH)}}$$

### Severity Rate ($SR$)
$$SR = \frac{\text{Total Lost Work Days} \times 200,000}{\text{Total Employee Hours Worked (EH)}}$$

## 3. Quantitative Risk Assessment Matrix ($5 \times 5$)
$$\text{Risk Score (RS)} = \text{Likelihood (1 to 5)} \times \text{Severity (1 to 5)}$$

* **1–4 (Low)**: Acceptable with standard operating controls.
* **5–9 (Medium)**: Planned reduction required; administrative and engineering controls.
* **10–16 (High)**: Urgent mitigation required before work proceeds.
* **17–25 (Critical / Extreme)**: Immediate shutdown of operations.

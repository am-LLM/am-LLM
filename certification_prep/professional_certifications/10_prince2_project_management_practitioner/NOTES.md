## 2. Earned Value Management (EVM) Formulas

* **Cost Variance ($CV$)**: $CV = EV - AC$ *(Positive = Under Budget)*
* **Schedule Variance ($SV$)**: $SV = EV - PV$ *(Positive = Ahead of Schedule)*
* **Cost Performance Index ($CPI$)**: $CPI = \frac{EV}{AC}$ *(> 1.0 = Cost Efficient)*
* **Schedule Performance Index ($SPI$)**: $SPI = \frac{EV}{PV}$ *(> 1.0 = Schedule Efficient)*
* **Estimate at Completion ($EAC$)**: $EAC = \frac{BAC}{CPI}$
* **Estimate to Complete ($ETC$)**: $ETC = EAC - AC$
* **To-Complete Performance Index ($TCPI$)**: $TCPI = \frac{BAC - EV}{BAC - AC}$
